GLP-1 Price Index: August 2026

Updated August 2, 2026 — Verified pricing from 10 active telehealth providers

The cheapest verified GLP-1 this month is Oak at $133/mo for compounded semaglutide and $199/mo for compounded tirzepatide, both flat across all doses. Listed cash prices held steady across every active provider since the last verification pass in early June. Two providers with fully transparent, all-inclusive pricing now sit near the bottom of the range: Liv Body at $179/mo and FeelGood at $149/mo.

What Changed This Month

No active provider changed its listed cash price. Every rate below was re-checked against provider pages, and the verified dates run from May 28 to June 9. Nothing moved up or down.

The active comparison set stands at 10 providers. Liv Body and FeelGood carry verified, no-hidden-fee pricing that includes medication, consultations, and shipping in one number, which is why they read cheaper than programs that bill a membership on top of the drug.

Two names are not in the active listing this month. Noom Med and LifeMD were pulled from the public comparison. Their pricing stays on file for reference, but they are not part of this month's active count.

Cheapest GLP-1 Options Right Now

Cheapest compounded semaglutide: Oak at $133/mo (all doses priced the same, no membership or shipping fees)

Cheapest compounded tirzepatide: Oak at $199/mo (same flat-dose structure)

Cheapest brand-name access: LillyDirect at $299/mo (Zepbound KwikPen, 2.5mg, cash-pay direct from Eli Lilly, free delivery). Hims lists the Wegovy pill at a comparable $298/mo all-in once its $149 membership is added to the $149 medication cost.

Oak is the value floor for anyone comfortable with compounded medication, and the flat-dose pricing matters: your cost does not climb as your dose does. If you want brand-name Zepbound without insurance, LillyDirect is the manufacturer-direct route at $299/mo for the starting dose. FeelGood at $149/mo and Wellorithm at $147/mo are the next-cheapest compounded semaglutide options after Oak, and both fold consultations and shipping into the price.

The Bigger Picture

Compounded semaglutide from a no-fee provider now starts at $133/mo, and four active providers list a compounded semaglutide entry point under $190. Brand-name access through the manufacturer starts at $299/mo. That gap, roughly $166/mo between the cheapest compounded option and manufacturer-direct brand, is the core cost decision for most self-pay buyers right now.

Half the active providers still keep some pricing behind an intake form or price it from review-site data rather than a published rate, which is why six of the ten carry an "estimated" tag. The providers moving toward flat, all-inclusive numbers (Oak, Liv Body, FeelGood, LillyDirect) are the ones a buyer can price without submitting personal information first.
